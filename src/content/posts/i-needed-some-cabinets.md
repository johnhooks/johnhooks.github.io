I recently moved and needed to design a set of cabinets.

I tried drawing them by hand, but my sketches raised more questions for the cabinet maker
than they answered. I needed a proper drawing with exact dimensions to show what I had in
mind.

I used to be pretty good at Fusion 360, but it had been long enough that I would need to
learn my way around it again. I could do that, or I could try building something just for
me.

I picked the second one.

That became [Drafter](https://drafter.johnhooks.io), a small parametric modeller that runs
in the browser. You can sketch shapes, extrude them into solids, and sketch on the faces
of what you have built.

It also gave me a project to try [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec) on.
The UI takes some inspiration from Ableton, just for fun.

You can [try Drafter here](https://drafter.johnhooks.io). The
[code is on GitHub](https://github.com/johnhooks/drafter).
